📌 Graduation Project: Electronically Steerable Transceiver

This project focuses on the design and development of a 2.4 GHz Electronically Steerable Antenna Array (ESAA) utilizing a 4×4 Butler Matrix, incorporating an STM32F411CEU6 microcontroller and an nRF24L01+ transceiver, is designed for Internet, Communication, and Positioning applications. By leveraging a four-element antenna array, the design enables precise beam steering, enhancing localization accuracy while providing a wide-angle coverage of 240° in the azimuth plane. This work was conducted under the technical supervision of The Egyptian Space Agency.
My Contributions
- PCB Layout & Schematic Design (Altium Designer)
  • Designed and developed schematic diagrams and a multi-layer PCB layout (10 cm × 6 cm, 0.64 cm thickness) for the communication subsystem.
  • Integrated power management, an STM32F411CEU6 microcontroller, a 2.4 GHz nRF24L01+ transceiver, and RF switches, ensuring seamless system functionality.
  • Implemented The STM32F411CEU6, based on a high-performance ARM® Cortex®-M4 32-bit RISC core, operates at up to 100 MHz, providing efficient real-time processing for RF communication and beamforming control. The nRF24L01+, a 2.4 GHz transceiver featuring an embedded baseband protocol engine (Enhanced ShockBurst™) for reliable, low-power wireless communication.
  • Integrated the RFX2401C Power Amplifier, significantly improving transmission performance. By adding the RFX2401C at both ends of the link, the system achieves an extended range increase of 26~35 dB, enhancing signal integrity and communication stability.
  • Optimized signal routing by implementing controlled impedance traces and reducing parasitic capacitance and inductance, ensuring minimal RF signal loss and maintaining signal integrity across the multi-layer PCB design. The design incorporates ground planes, guard traces, and via stitching to enhance electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) and reduce crosstalk between RF components.
- Embedded Systems & MCU Software (Embedded C)
  • Developed firmware to manage RF switching and beamforming.
  • Implemented SPI, ADC, and GPIO protocols.
  • Optimized real-time processing and power management.
- Key Achievements
  • Compact, multi-layer PCB for RF integration.
  • Impedance matching for minimal signal loss.
  • Efficient voltage regulation for stable operation.
  • Seamless software-hardware integration for RF control.

This project demonstrates my expertise in PCB design, embedded systems, and RF communication for satellite applications.
